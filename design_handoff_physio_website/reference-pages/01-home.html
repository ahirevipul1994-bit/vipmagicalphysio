Who you'll be seeing

Care that starts with listening, not a template.

Dr. Vipul Ahire is a physiotherapist with approximately six years of clinical experience, practising in Shirur. His approach is straightforward: assess properly, explain clearly, and build a plan that fits the life the patient actually lives.

A note on what we don't claim. We don't publish invented ratings, borrowed testimonials, or recovery statistics we can't verify. What we can offer is an honest assessment, a clear explanation, and a plan that is reviewed and adjusted.

1

Individual assessment

Every patient is examined, not categorised. The plan follows the findings.

2

Patient-centred

Goals are set around your day — stairs, work, walking, caring for family.

3

Rehabilitation-focused

The aim is function and independence, not endless appointments.

4

Exercise and education

Active treatment, with the reasoning explained so you can self-manage.

5

Honest about limits

If your problem needs medical or surgical review, we say so and refer.

6

Family included

With consent, caregivers are taught how to assist safely between visits.

What to expect

How treatment actually works here.

Step one

Assessment

A conversation about your history and daily demands, followed by examination of movement, strength and function. Roughly 45 minutes. You leave with an explanation, not just a diagnosis.

Step two

Your plan

Two or three specific things to work on, with the reasoning explained. Exercises are chosen for your current capacity and taught until you can do them correctly alone.

Step three

Review

Progress is checked against real tasks — stairs, walking distance, sleep — and the programme is adjusted. If it isn't working, we change it rather than repeat it.

When needed

Referral

Some problems need imaging, medication or surgery. Physiotherapy is not the answer to everything, and recognising that early is part of good care.
