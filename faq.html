Physiotherapy

A physiotherapist assesses movement, strength and function, then designs and teaches a rehabilitation programme. Treatment is mostly active — exercise, movement retraining and education — supported where appropriate by hands-on techniques.

In most cases, no. You can book directly. If your situation needs medical assessment first, we will tell you and help you get it.

It depends on the problem and how you progress. We do not pre-sell fixed packages. Most programmes involve a small number of appointments spread out, with independent work in between.

Some techniques may be uncomfortable briefly. Exercises often produce mild soreness afterwards, which is normal. Sharp pain is not necessary for progress and should be reported.
