Who may benefit

Good for some things. Honest about the rest.

Well suited to remote care

  • Follow-up reviews of an existing programme
  • Exercise progression and technique correction
  • Self-management advice for stable, long-standing problems
  • Guidance before deciding whether to travel for assessment
  • Continuity while travelling or away from Shirur
  • Patients who cannot attend in person for practical reasons

Needs in-person assessment

  • Problems where hands-on testing is needed to diagnose
  • Neurological symptoms needing examination
  • Severe, rapidly worsening or unexplained pain
  • Post-operative reviews requiring wound or swelling assessment
  • Suspected fracture, dislocation or significant injury
  • Any situation where the video call cannot show us enough

How online consultation works

  1. Choose a consultation type. Initial assessment, follow-up, or exercise programme review.
  2. Select a preferred time. You propose; we confirm. There is no live calendar behind this page.
  3. Send your details. Name, mobile, and a short description of the problem.
  4. Receive confirmation. We confirm the slot and send joining instructions by phone or WhatsApp.
  5. Attend the consultation. Video call, with movement observation and demonstration.
  6. Receive follow-up guidance. A written summary with your exercises and review point.

What to prepare

  • A private room and a stable connection
  • Comfortable clothing you can move in
  • A chair, and space to take a few steps
  • Any reports, scans or prescriptions
  • A wall or sturdy furniture within reach for balance tests
  • A family member, if you would like support

How exercise demonstration works

We demonstrate each exercise on camera, then watch you perform it from two angles where the room allows. Most bodyweight and household-object exercises can be taught effectively this way. Where an exercise cannot be performed safely without hands-on setup, we will leave it out and say why.

Limitations, stated plainly

We cannot palpate tissue, test joint play, assess subtle strength differences by hand, or examine reflexes and sensation reliably. These are real limits, not formalities. When your presentation depends on them, the correct advice is an in-person appointment — and that is what you will get.

Consent and privacy

Consent is required before a consultation, and is recorded in the booking request. Consultations are not recorded. Medical details are not stored on this website; the booking form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to send. Please avoid sharing sensitive medical information over unsecured channels.
